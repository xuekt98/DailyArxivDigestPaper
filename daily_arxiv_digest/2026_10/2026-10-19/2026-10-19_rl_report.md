### 1. Grounded Credit Assignment in Reinforcement Learning for Multi-Turn Search Agents

**Abstract**

> Outcome-reward reinforcement learning such as GRPO gives every token of a search-agent rollout the same advantage, although the rollout exercises two capabilities: finding evidence and using it.
